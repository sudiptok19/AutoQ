# AutoQ-Trace engineering master plan

**Review state.** This is the team’s proposed v2 engineering baseline. [CONTRACT_ALIGNMENT.md](CONTRACT_ALIGNMENT.md) records the five joint decisions required to reconcile it with main and the published scanner. [WORK_SPLIT.md](WORK_SPLIT.md) assigns implementation files. This branch contains planning documents only.

## Product invariant

At each irreversible step the server must be able to explain which units are moving, which evidence authorized them, and which current holds or order constraints apply. Packing requires valid unit authorization. Sealing binds a particular package revision to its contents. Loading additionally requires an eligible order, valid package identity and a fresh intact recheck. Departure revalidates every loaded package and finalizes a reconciled manifest. A valid signature or a favorable vision score alone cannot authorize movement.

The prototype covers one site, serialized components, one part per lot and one order line per package. A package may mix lots of the same part. Retain every original material, certificate, unit, inspection, passport, packing, scan, movement and destination link needed to reconstruct a recall. The phone and physical sample objects are the reference test setup.

## Release, containment and genealogy

1. **Freeze the lot.** Record supplier, material heat, policy version, production sequence and membership. New prototype lots have one heat. Preserve older multi-heat links for trace queries. Freeze selected sample IDs before inspection. Late production enters another lot.
2. **Choose the inspection basis.** A GREEN non-A lot may release on its complete passing frozen sample. A missing or failed selected result holds release. Every A-part and every unit affected during RED requires direct individual PASS. A sampled passport must identify exactly which units were inspected through its authorizing inspection IDs. The unsampled unit’s own inspection history remains truthful.
3. **Handle a 4M change.** Record category, planned status, actor, reason, effective time, scoped serials and the applied versioned policy. Each event creates its own run and obligations, including required rechecks of existing inventory. Enroll subsequent affected units explicitly. Only qualifying individual PASS on distinct units in production order increments that run’s counter. Duplicate/retry counts once. HOLD leaves the unit unresolved. REJECT resets credit to zero in a fresh sequence epoch and preserves previous evidence.
4. **Clear with accountability.** Keep new passports and activation blocked throughout RED, including READY_FOR_CLEARANCE. Require the configured threshold, completed obligations, and a quality manager’s explicit clearance with reason. Evaluate every applicable run/recall independently. Persist all hold and release transitions. A manager may send a HOLD for reinspection or reject it; passing evidence is still required.
5. **Issue immutable credentials.** Use AQ1 Ed25519 with a key ID, explicit type/version and deterministic bytes. Each signed PP binds serial, lot/part/heat genealogy, exact authorizing IDs, evidence digests, inspection basis, 4M tag and issue time. Keep private keys server-side. After 4M clearance, directly inspected affected units carry INDIVIDUAL_100PCT and four_m_tagged=true. Supersede an obsolete sampled passport and link revisions. Verify signature, trusted key, server identity, superseding and live blockers independently. PKG and SNAP have separate validated payloads. Exact fields and legacy handling are in alignment decision 3.

## Physical inspection, seals and process drift

| Feature | Engineering rule | Evidence needed to accept it |
|---|---|---|
| Component inspection | Run image decoding, resolution, blur/glare and registration checks before a calibrated OpenCV reference difference. Save original bytes, aligned image, method/profile version and difference image where available. Emit raw score, regions and reason codes. Central policy alone maps measurements to PASS/HOLD/REJECT. | Use separate phone capture sessions for reference/calibration and held-out images of clean, visibly defective, blurry, glaring and misaligned samples. Publish sample counts, false accepts, false rejects and unverifiable captures by condition. |
| Physical seal | Enroll a patterned seal across the package seam. Match features, use RANSAC homography and compute SSIM on the registered seam. Keep the reference, probe, revision and metrics. Distinguish INTACT, SUSPICIOUS and UNVERIFIABLE. Missing texture, poor lighting, inadequate registration or ambiguous similarity requires a recheck. | Capture intact, torn/displaced and unreadable cases on the actual package. Repacking/resealing invalidates old checks and PKG identity. Race re-enrollment with verification to prove the wrong revision cannot authorize load. |
| SPC drift | Scope frozen baselines to part, station and method version. A line maps to one station in the prototype. Cold start/low variance are explicit states with unavailable normalized statistics. Once ready, evaluate upper CUSUM and a five-rising-scores rule on ordered distinct valid measurements. Persist suspected drift for QC review. | Show baseline, threshold and measured trigger index for stable and drifting sequences. Repeat with stale version, duplicate and low variance. Label synthetic sequences; they establish rule behavior, while phone evidence establishes optical performance. |

Thresholds are versioned demo parameters until evaluated on held-out data. A recalibration creates a new profile version. Changing a label on a fixture cannot manufacture a passing production inspection or seal.

## Shipping, offline and recall

- **Packing and physical state.** Enforce one current package membership per serial and record membership intervals. Preserve hold as an overlay on physical location. LOAD_ACCEPTED, DEPARTED and DELIVERED are separately attributable facts. A prepared or closed manifest is still on site until a departure is recorded.
- **Commit order.** Loading, departure, quarantine and applicable 4M/packing changes use PostgreSQL transactions. Lock affected heat, lot, unit, package, order and shipment rows in that order, sorted by ID in each class. Re-read mutable memberships under lock. Enforce unique active loads, quantity reconciliation and idempotent scan results. Persist denied attempts with reasons. Prepare via the existing close endpoint, then finalize manifest digest and DEPARTED atomically after rechecking all gates.
- **Recall.** Count distinct affected units by at-plant, loaded-not-departed, in-transit and delivered state from actual accepted loads and movements. Separately identify all affected whole packages. A held mixed package may contain clean units. Quarantine first means block departure and create removal work; departure first means in-transit consignee action. Delivered stock gets a customer notification task. Track completion, actor, timestamp and evidence.
- **Offline.** The trusted HTTPS PWA verifies a short-lived, signed, scoped SNAP and package signature, then records a provisional staging decision in IndexedDB. Expired or missing trust material cannot authorize staging. Sync ordered events through the live dispatch gate. Bind device/operator, verify contiguous sequence and chain against the server checkpoint, compare repeated event content and commit results and checkpoint together. A new recall or competing load may reject a locally staged package. Persist segregation work and show the resulting physical action. Resolve pending devices before manifest finalization.
- **Live signals.** Opus writes business facts, audit records and outbox events in the same transaction. After commit its relay calls Astra’s schema-validated, scope-aware broadcaster. The control room can receive seal, duplicate, quarantine, 4M and drift alerts with stable IDs, severity and an audible flag. REST catch-up and client deduplication cover dropped connections. Opus owns auth, alert lifecycle, presentation, sound and operator controls.

## Platform, reporting and ownership

Use one FastAPI/Python 3.12 backend with PostgreSQL 16, SQLAlchemy/Alembic, immutable SHA-256 evidence storage, and a Vite/React phone and desktop app. Keep the live client’s existing REST vocabulary where possible. Shared errors contain a code, message and actionable details; record request IDs, actor and policy version where applicable. Unknown keys, missing evidence, invalid calibration, stale seals, unavailable dependencies and unresolved holds produce explicit errors, HOLD or BLOCKED. Mark live, prerecorded evaluation and synthetic evidence distinctly. Never substitute demo data on a live failure.

Generate D1–D8 locally from a frozen evidence snapshot. Separate facts, hypotheses, proposed actions and pending review. Preserve exact recall counts, evidence references and source time in both JSON and deterministic downloadable PDF. Root cause, effectiveness and closure remain pending until supported and reviewed. The report is a draft until audited manager approval.

## Delivery and release proof

1. **Foundation.** Establish real DB migrations, authentication, evidence storage and the trusted LAN origin. Test the phone camera, certificate trust and a protected original download. Collect separate calibration and held-out physical images.
2. **Inspection and release.** Prove sampled GREEN and individual A-part release, reject wrong-unit evidence, exercise duplicate/HOLD/REJECT/overlap recovery and prove RED cannot mint. Decode real signed outputs in Python and the browser.
3. **Packaging through recall.** Verify seal revisions and mixed-lot lineage. Race two loaders and quarantine versus departure using separate real PostgreSQL connections. Check exact counts, single load, correct physical work and post-commit alerts.
4. **Offline.** Reload a disconnected production PWA, reconnect and replay twice. Exercise stale snapshots, a broken chain, a lost online response and two devices competing for one package. Confirm durable segregation and no duplicate credits.
5. **Reports and handoff.** Validate SPC triggers, evidence-linked PDF pages and report counts against the source snapshot. Run a live browser journey and a physical phone-to-desktop alert. Publish setup, reproducible seed, public contract artifacts, demo walkthrough, measurements and an honest capability matrix.

Prototype boundaries include single-site deployment, single-process WS transport and real phone validation. PatchCore/ONNX, aftermarket one-time tokens, print-copy detection, multi-site federation and LLM-written reports remain future work. No OEM endorsement, certification, plant policy or unmeasured performance target is implied.
